Two paths to living forever

Fix the cells, or swap the parts.

Path 1 · Biology

Repair cells

  • Slow or reverse aging inside the body you have
  • Bryan Johnson made “Don’t Die” a public project for it
  • Most “live forever” talk lives here
vs
Path 2 · Bionics

Replace parts

  • Swap failing organs and limbs for engineered ones
  • Upgrade as the tech improves
  • Far fewer people are talking about it

The two paths stack: biology buys time; bionics replace what wears out.

Bottlenecks

Five things standing in the way.

01

Nerve-interface bandwidth

Surface EMG gives a few noisy channels. Implants do better, but still only dozens of channels.

02

Rejection & infection

Pig organs fight antibody rejection. Implants fail by infection and scarring.

03

Power

Hearts and powered legs need batteries or external drivers. Less outside the body means harder power and heat.

04

Surgery scale

Nerve and bone-anchoring surgeries happen at a handful of specialist centers.

05

Cost

$30K+

for a robotic leg today.

Source: Frog’s Bionic Body Roadmap, “Cross-cutting pressure points” · cost: robotic leg pricing per roadmap sources; iSens explant: J NeuroEng Rehabil 2025
